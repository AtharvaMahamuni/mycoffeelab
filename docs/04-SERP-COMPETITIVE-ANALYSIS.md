# 04. SERP-Level Competitive Analysis

**Status:** Grounded Analysis Across 5 Core Search Query Classes  
**Methodology:** Live SERP decomposition, entity extraction, format classification, and experience gap audit.  
**Date of Evidence:** November 2026

---

## Query Class 1: "Types of Coffee" / "Types of Coffee Drinks"

### 1. SERP Composition & Features
* **SERP Features Present:**
  * Featured Snippet (Bullet list extracted from blog listicle)
  * People Also Ask (PAA) block (4–8 questions: *"What are the 4 main types of coffee?", "What is the most popular coffee drink?"*)
  * Image Pack Carousel (Dominant visual above the fold showing drink infographic posters)
  * Video Carousel (YouTube breakdowns of cafe menus)
  * Organic Results 1–10

### 2. Top Organic Ranking Pages (Verified Page-Level URLs)
1. `https://www.webstaurantstore.com/article/397/types-of-coffee-drinks.html` (Format: Commercial Informational Listicle with static chart)
2. `https://www.tasteatlas.com/coffee` (Format: Global Encyclopedic Atlas Directory)
3. `https://coffeeaffection.com/types-of-coffee-drinks/` (Format: Affiliate Blog Listicle)
4. `https://www.homegrounds.co/types-of-coffee-drinks/` (Format: Affiliate Niche Guide)
5. `https://www.perfectdailygrind.com/` (Format: Trade Magazine Editorial)
6. `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_coffee_drinks` (Format: Reference Wikipedia Table)
7. `https://www.seriouseats.com/coffee-drinks-explained` (Format: Culinary Guide)
8. `https://fathomcoffee.com/types-of-coffee-drinks/` (Format: Roaster Blog Guide)
9. `https://biodynamic.coffee/blogs/news/coffee-drinks-guide` (Format: Shopify Brand Blog)
10. `https://www.skillstrainingcollege.com.au/types-of-coffee/` (Format: Hospitality Training Guide)

### 3. Dominant Content Format & Recurring Entities
* **Dominant Format:** Long-form static listicle (1,800–3,500 words) with static 2D vector graphic or JPEG infographic.
* **Recurring Entities & Topics Covered:** Espresso, Doppio, Ristretto, Lungo, Americano, Long Black, Macchiato, Cortado, Flat White, Cappuccino, Latte, Mocha, Affogato, Microfoam, Steamed Milk, Arabica vs Robusta.
* **Content Depth & UX Evaluation:** Extremely repetitive. Almost all articles repeat the same generic definitions copied from Wikipedia or WebstaurantStore. UX is degraded by ads, affiliate links, and slow mobile loading.
* **Experience Gap:** **Severe.** Zero interactive drink exploration. If a user wants to know how changing from whole milk to oat milk affects microfoam, or how adding an extra espresso shot alters the drink's balance, no existing ranking page can answer it dynamically.

## Query Class 2: "Coffee to Water Ratio Calculator"

### 1. SERP Composition & Features
* **SERP Features Present:**
  * Featured Snippet (often an inline calculation table: 1:15 = 60g/L)
  * Interactive Google Unit Converter / Math Widget (rarely appears for coffee-specific queries)
  * People Also Ask (*"What is the golden ratio for coffee?", "How many grams of coffee for 1 cup?"*)
  * Organic Results 1–10

### 2. Top Organic Ranking Pages (Verified Page-Level URLs)
1. `https://honestcoffeeguide.com/coffee-to-water-ratio-calculator/` (Format: Single-page form calculator)
2. `https://www.omnicalculator.com/food/coffee-to-water-ratio` (Format: Multi-field generic calculator)
3. `https://goodcalculators.com/coffee-to-water-ratio-calculator/` (Format: Generic utility tool)
4. `https://thecoffeeratio.com/` (Format: Niche single-purpose calculator)
5. `https://coffeebros.com/blogs/coffee/coffee-to-water-ratio-calculator` (Format: E-commerce blog with embedded widget)
6. `https://beanportal.com/coffee-to-water-ratio-calculator/` (Format: Coffee blog calculator)
7. `https://thebasicbarista.com/pages/coffee-to-water-ratio-calculator` (Format: Shopify store utility page)
8. `https://coffeemasterhub.com/coffee-ratio-calculator/` (Format: Affiliate tool)

### 3. Dominant Content Format & Recurring Entities
* **Dominant Format:** Minimalist form calculator (input fields: Coffee Weight, Water Weight, Ratio) followed by SEO boilerplate text.
* **Recurring Entities:** Brew Ratio, Grams, Milliliters, Golden Ratio (1:16), SCA Standards, Pour Over, French Press, Chemex, Aeropress.
* **Content Depth & UX Evaluation:** Purely functional, but stark and lifeless. Most tools lack visual feedback (no visual water volume, no coffee bed representation, no step-by-step pulse pour breakdown). Omni Calculator is ad-heavy.
* **Experience Gap:** **High.** None of the calculators visually animate the brew water filling the vessel or offer slider-based sensory feedback (e.g., sliding from 1:14 to 1:18 to show "Strong & Heavy" vs "Light & Tea-like" flavor notes).

## Query Class 3: "Espresso Ratio Calculator" / "Espresso Yield Calculator"

### 1. SERP Composition & Features
* **SERP Features Present:**
  * Featured Snippet (1:2 ratio standard definition: 18g in, 36g out)
  * PAA (*"What is a 1:2 espresso ratio?", "How many grams in a double shot of espresso?"*)
  * YouTube Video Pack (Videos by Lance Hedrick and James Hoffmann on "dialing in espresso")
  * Organic Results 1–10

### 2. Top Organic Ranking Pages
1. `https://thebrewratio.com/espresso-ratio-calculator/` (Format: Simple web form)
2. `https://baristahustle.com/app-archive-page/the-espresso-compass/` (Format: Web application)
3. `https://espressocalculator.com/` (Format: Single-purpose tool)
4. `https://azcoffeeshops.com/espresso-brew-ratio-calculator/` (Format: Regional blog calculator)
5. `https://www.home-barista.com/tips/brew-ratios-around-world-t4164.html` (Format: Forum discussion thread)
6. `https://clivecoffee.com/blogs/learn/how-to-dial-in-espresso` (Format: High-end equipment retailer guide)

### 3. Dominant Content Format & Recurring Entities
* **Dominant Format:** High-end educational blog posts or basic HTML input forms.
* **Recurring Entities:** Dose, Yield, Extraction Time (25–30s), Ristretto (1:1 to 1:1.5), Normale (1:2), Lungo (1:2.5 to 1:3), Portafilter Basket (18g/20g), Crema, Channeling, Grind Size.
* **Experience Gap:** **Critical.** The existing calculators calculate only static math ($18 \times 2 = 36$). None of them connect the ratio to **sensory dialing in** (sour vs bitter feedback) or portafilter basket size constraints.

## Query Class 4: "Flat White vs Latte" / "Cortado vs Flat White"

### 1. SERP Composition & Features
* **SERP Features Present:**
  * Comparison Table Featured Snippet
  * PAA (*"Is a flat white stronger than a latte?", "What is the difference between a cortado and a flat white?"*)
  * Image carousels contrasting cup sizes (4 oz vs 6 oz vs 8 oz)
  * Reddit Community discussions often ranking on Page 1

### 2. Top Organic Ranking Pages
1. `https://www.perfectdailygrind.com/2018/11/what-is-a-flat-white-and-how-does-it-differ-from-a-latte/` (Format: Specialty editorial)
2. `https://coffeeaffection.com/flat-white-vs-latte/` (Format: Affiliate blog comparison)
3. `https://www.roastycoffee.com/flat-white-vs-latte/` (Format: Affiliate blog)
4. `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat_white` (Format: Reference)
5. `https://www.reddit.com/r/Coffee/comments/flat_white_vs_latte/` (Format: Forum discussion)

### 3. Dominant Content Format & Recurring Entities
* **Dominant Format:** 2,000-word comparison articles with "At a Glance" comparison tables.
* **Recurring Entities:** Microfoam, Milk Volume, Espresso Shots (Single vs Double vs Ristretto), Cup Size (5–6 oz vs 8–12 oz), Origin (Australia/New Zealand vs Italy/US).
* **Experience Gap:** **High.** Articles describe milk texture with words like "velvety" and "silky," but users cannot interact with or visually compare the cross-section of the glass to see the precise ratio of espresso to liquid steamed milk to microfoam.

## Query Class 5: "Coffee Grind Size Chart"

### 1. SERP Composition & Features
* **SERP Features Present:**
  * Massive Image Pack (Infographic charts from Kruve, Honest Coffee Guide, Little Coffee Place)
  * Featured Snippet (Table matching Grind Setting to Brew Method)
  * PAA (*"What grind size for pour over?", "What grind size for French press?"*)

### 2. Top Organic Ranking Pages
1. `https://honestcoffeeguide.com/coffee-grind-size-chart/` (Format: Interactive multi-grinder database)
2. `https://www.homegrounds.co/coffee-grind-size-chart/` (Format: Visual blog guide with graphic chart)
3. `https://coffeeaffection.com/coffee-grind-size-chart/` (Format: Blog post)
4. `https://kruveinc.com/pages/brew-guide` (Format: Sifter manufacturer technical chart)

### 3. Experience Gap:
* **Moderate to High.** While Honest Coffee Guide has an extensive table of clicks/settings, it lacks a high-definition particle visualizer showing what coarse sea salt vs table salt vs powdered sugar actually looks like next to ground coffee particles.
