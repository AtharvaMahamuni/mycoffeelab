# 07. User Intent and Audience Analysis

**Core Premise:** Coffee searchers are not a monolithic demographic. A college student trying to order at a specialty cafe has radically different needs from a home barista trying to eliminate sour channeling in a light-roast Ethiopian espresso shot.

---

## 1. Audience Segmentation Matrix

### 1. Coffee Beginners
* **Main Motivations:** Overcome cafe menu anxiety; understand the basics without feeling judged; make drink choices with confidence.
* **Questions They Want Answered:** *"What's the difference between a latte and a cappuccino?", "Is an Americano just black coffee?", "What does a macchiato taste like?"*
* **Problems Encountered:** Confusing Italian terminology; ordering the wrong drink (e.g., expecting a sweet Starbucks caramel drink and receiving a 2 oz traditional espresso macchiato); conflicting explanations online.
* **Most Relevant Keywords:** `types of coffee`, `coffee drinks explained`, `latte vs cappuccino`, `what is a cortado`.
* **Preferred Experience:** Clean, friendly, visual infographics; interactive cross-sections; plain-English glossaries.
* **Repeat-Usage Behaviour:** Low to moderate initially; returns when visiting new cafes or exploring menus.

---

### 2. Casual Coffee Drinkers
* **Main Motivations:** Upgrade their daily morning routine; brew better coffee at home using affordable kitchen equipment (drip maker, French press, Nespresso).
* **Questions They Want Answered:** *"How many scoops of coffee for 4 cups?", "Why does my French press coffee taste bitter?", "Best coffee-to-water ratio for standard drip machine."*
* **Problems Encountered:** Measuring coffee with inaccurate kitchen tablespoons; stale pre-ground coffee; overly bitter or watery cups.
* **Most Relevant Keywords:** `coffee to water ratio`, `french press coffee ratio`, `how much coffee for 4 cups`, `why is my coffee bitter`.
* **Preferred Experience:** Simple, instant calculators that accept both cups/mugs and grams; fast brewing tips without scientific jargon.
* **Repeat-Usage Behaviour:** Moderate; bookmarks the ratio calculator for morning brewing.

---

### 3. Home Coffee Enthusiasts & Pour-Over Brewers
* **Main Motivations:** Craft exceptional, cafe-quality pour-overs (V60, Chemex, Kalita); unlock delicate floral and fruity terroir notes from single-origin beans.
* **Questions They Want Answered:** *"What ratio for Ethiopian light roast on V60?", "How much bloom water should I use?", "How do I grind for Chemex vs V60?"*
* **Problems Encountered:** Inconsistent brew times, stalls in the filter paper, astringent finish, underextraction.
* **Most Relevant Keywords:** `v60 brew calculator`, `chemex ratio calculator`, `coffee bloom ratio`, `coffee grind size chart`.
* **Preferred Experience:** Precision mass-based calculators with pulse-pour stage breakdowns; visual particle size charts; extraction troubleshooting cards.
* **Repeat-Usage Behaviour:** High; visits daily or weekly when opening a new bag of beans.

---

### 4. Espresso Enthusiasts ("Home Baristas")
* **Main Motivations:** Master the demanding craft of home espresso; eliminate channeling; dial in expensive specialty beans on semi-automatic machines (Gaggia, Breville, Lelit, La Marzocco).
* **Questions They Want Answered:** *"My shot runs 36g in 18 seconds and tastes sour, what do I adjust?", "Best ratio for medium-dark vs light roast espresso", "What basket size for 18g dose?"*
* **Problems Encountered:** Puck channeling, sour underextraction, inconsistent grinder retention, temperature fluctuations, confusing fluid ounce recipes.
* **Most Relevant Keywords:** `espresso ratio calculator`, `espresso dial in guide`, `sour vs bitter espresso`, `ristretto ratio`, `1:2 espresso recipe`.
* **Preferred Experience:** Interactive dial-in simulators; dose-yield-time matrix; troubleshooting sliders; zero fluff.
* **Repeat-Usage Behaviour:** Extremely high; actively uses dial-in tools during daily workflow adjustments.

---

### 5. Alternative & Cold Brewers
* **Main Motivations:** Make smooth, low-acid cold brew concentrate; experiment with Aeropress recipes; use Moka pots without burning the coffee.
* **Questions They Want Answered:** *"What ratio for cold brew concentrate?", "How long to steep French press in fridge?", "Aeropress inverted vs standard ratio."*
* **Problems Encountered:** Muddy cold brew filtration, sludge in French press, metallic taste from Moka pots.
* **Most Relevant Keywords:** `cold brew ratio calculator`, `aeropress recipe ratio`, `moka pot ratio`, `french press brew time`.
* **Preferred Experience:** Method-specific interactive sliders (Concentrate vs Ready-to-Drink); step-by-step visual timers.
* **Repeat-Usage Behaviour:** High during summer months (cold brew batch preparation).

---

### 6. Baristas & Aspiring Hospitality Professionals
* **Main Motivations:** Standardize cafe drink builds; memorize traditional vs modern recipes; train new cafe staff.
* **Questions They Want Answered:** *"Standard microfoam thickness for flat white vs cappuccino", "Exact milk volume in 6 oz cappuccino", "How to calculate extraction yield without a refractometer."*
* **Problems Encountered:** Disagreements with customers on drink definitions (e.g., Australian vs UK flat white); lack of standardized visual training tools.
* **Most Relevant Keywords:** `flat white standard recipe`, `specialty coffee drink specs`, `sca brewing standards`, `extraction yield formula`.
* **Preferred Experience:** Authoritative, high-density reference cards; exportable/printable recipe specs; visual ratio guides.
* **Repeat-Usage Behaviour:** Regular reference tool during shifts and training sessions.

---

### 7. Culinary Students & Coffee Science Enthusiasts
* **Main Motivations:** Understand the chemistry of coffee: Maillard reactions, caramelization, extraction dynamics, and organic acids.
* **Questions They Want Answered:** *"What compounds extract first during brewing?", "How does water temperature change acid extraction?", "Why does hard water mute acidity?"*
* **Problems Encountered:** Lack of interactive simulations; research papers locked behind academic paywalls.
* **Most Relevant Keywords:** `coffee extraction chemistry`, `coffee tds explained`, `organic acids in coffee extraction`.
* **Preferred Experience:** Interactive extraction simulations, visual component timelines, interactive solubility curves.
* **Repeat-Usage Behaviour:** Moderate to high during study and research phases.

## 2. Search Intent to Product Experience Mapping

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        INTENT-TO-EXPERIENCE CONVERSION FUNNEL                          │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. QUERY: "what is a flat white"
   ├─ User Expectation: Immediate visual and text definition of what they will receive in a cup.
   ├─ Current SERP Solution: 2,500-word blog post on the history of coffee in Sydney vs Melbourne with 15 banner ads.
   ├─ Unmet Need: Direct, instant visual comparison of espresso, steamed milk, and microfoam thickness in a 6 oz ceramic cup.
   └─ MyCoffeeLab Solution: Instant 2D/3D visual cup cross-section + 3-bullet recipe summary + interactive "Compare to Latte" button.

2. QUERY: "coffee to water ratio calculator"
   ├─ User Expectation: Type in grams of coffee or target mug size and get the exact water amount instantly.
   ├─ Current SERP Solution: Clunky multi-step form surrounded by auto-play video ads and affiliate banners.
   ├─ Unmet Need: Tactile, real-time reactive slider that also illustrates the brew vessel and bloom water phase.
   └─ MyCoffeeLab Solution: Zero-friction interactive slider (Coffee ↔ Water ↔ Ratio) with auto-updating visual pour guide and bloom timer.

3. QUERY: "espresso tastes sour"
   ├─ User Expectation: Urgent troubleshooting while standing at their espresso machine with a bad shot in hand.
   ├─ Current SERP Solution: Lengthy essays explaining coffee bean origins and roast levels before getting to troubleshooting.
   ├─ Unmet Need: A dynamic, interactive diagnostic tool: "Tastes Sour? → Increase Yield or Grind Finer."
   └─ MyCoffeeLab Solution: Interactive Sensory Compass: Select "Sour / Sharp" → Visual slider shows recommended yield increase (+4g) with live explanatory feedback.
```
