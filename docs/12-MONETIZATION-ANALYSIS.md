# 12. Monetisation and Business Potential

**Core Economic Thesis:** Traffic alone does not generate revenue. Commercial viability depends on the precise alignment between **user intent**, **contextual relevance**, and **trust-preserving monetization models**.

## 3. Four Viable Revenue Streams for MyCoffeeLab

### Stream 1: Curated Equipment Affiliates (Primary Early Revenue)
* **Mechanics:** When a user is using the V60 brewing calculator or the Espresso Dial-In tool, discrete contextual chips appear below the tool: *"Tools Used for this Recipe: Hario V60 Plastic 02, Timemore Black Mirror Scale, 1Zpresso J-Max Grinder."*
* **Partner Networks:** Specialty retailers with higher commission rates and longer cookie windows than Amazon:
  - Prima Coffee Equipment (5–8% commission)
  - Fellow Products Affiliate Program (8–10% commission)
  - Eight Ounce Coffee / Seattle Coffee Gear (5–7% commission)
  - Amazon Associates (Secondary fallback for mass consumer items, 3–4% commission)
* **Estimated Economics:** A single espresso machine or premium grinder conversion ($400–$1,200 AOV) generates $25 to $90 in net commission. 100 conversions/month produces $3,500–$7,000 monthly recurring affiliate revenue.

### Stream 2: Specialty Roaster Drops & Bean Discovery (Medium-Term)
* **Mechanics:** The platform features a curated **"Bean of the Month / Dial-In Companion"**. When users explore single-origin recipes (e.g., dialing in a natural processed Ethiopian bean), we feature specific specialty roasters whose beans fit the exact roast profile.
* **Partner Models:** Direct affiliate links (10–15% on bean purchases) or flat-fee sponsored features from vetted independent roasters (e.g., Onyx, Square Mile, Tim Wendelboe, Counter Culture).
* **User Value:** Enhances the learning experience by recommending freshly roasted beans that actually work with the recipes shown.

### Stream 3: Digital Products & Premium Coffee Lab Features (Long-Term)
* **Mechanics:** Keep all core calculators and drink guides 100% free and open. Introduce an optional **"MyCoffeeLab Pro"** digital utility package:
  - Cloud synchronization of custom recipes across devices.
  - Printable/laminated high-resolution PDF Barista Station Cheat Sheets.
  - Water chemistry mineralization calculator (custom magnesium/bicarbonate mixing).
  - Exportable cafe recipe sheets with custom logo for coffee shop training.
* **Pricing Model:** \$19 one-time lifetime pass or \$3/month micro-subscription.

### Stream 4: Discrete Direct Brand Sponsorships (Replacing Programmatic Ads)
* **Mechanics:** Instead of 20 blinking programmatic banners, we offer **one single, high-taste, static sponsor slot** per page category (e.g., *"Precision Brewing Powered by Acaia Scales"* or *"Water Quality Supported by Third Wave Water"*).
* **Economics:** Flat monthly sponsorship fees ($500–$2,000/month per category) yielding higher net revenue than programmatic CPMs while preserving an immaculate, magazine-grade aesthetic.

## 4. What We Strictly Reject
* **No Intrusive Ad Networks:** No Mediavine, Raptive, Ezoic, or Google AdSense banners. Ad clutter destroys the tactile, professional credibility required for an interactive laboratory tool.
* **No Gated Calculators:** Never require an email address or account creation to calculate a brew ratio. Utility must be instant and friction-free to maximize word-of-mouth adoption.
