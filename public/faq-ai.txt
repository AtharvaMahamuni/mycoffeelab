Q: What is the SCA golden cup ratio?
A: 55 g of coffee per litre of water, plus or minus 10%. The center is about 1:18.2.

Q: Is 1:16 the SCA standard?
A: No. 1:16 is a common pour-over preset. It is slightly stronger than the SCA band.

Q: Is bloom water extra?
A: No. Bloom is twice the coffee dose, and it is part of the total water.

Q: Does MyCoffeeLab show caffeine or energy figures?
A: No.
